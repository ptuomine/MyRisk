    How a turn works

    1. Draft: tap your regions to place new troops. You get 1 troop for every 2 regions you own (at least 3), plus the bonus of each continent you hold. The Drafts column shows how many are left.
    2. Tap Attack in the turn bar when your troops are placed. The highlighted step is the usual next one.
    3. Attack: tap one of your regions. The enemy regions you can attack light up. Tap one, then Attack! Dashed lines across the sea are routes that count as borders.
    4. Move (optional): tap one of your regions, then any lit-up region of yours. Every region you can reach through your own regions (by land or sea route) lights up. Each tap moves one troop. Tap the first region again to pick a different one. No attacks after moving.
    5. End Turn to hand over to the next player.

    Computer level: Easy makes mistakes and attacks cautiously. Normal plays solidly. Hard keeps attacking, goes after whoever is ahead and blocks you from completing continents. No level cheats: all get the same troops and rules as you. The choice applies from the next computer turn.

    Computer players take their turns by themselves. Every move appears under Moves, newest first.

    Hold a whole continent at the start of your turn to get its bonus troops (the number next to its name).

    A battle uses no dice: every attacking troop except one cancels one defender. Conquer at least one region in a turn and you earn a card.

    Cards: during Draft, trade 3 cards for extra troops with Trade cards. A set is three of a kind or one of each type (Infantry 2, Cavalry 3, Artillery 4; a Wild card counts as any type). The button shows how many troops you get.

    Knock a player out and you take all their cards. If that gives you a set, you can trade right away, even mid-attack: place the new troops, then tap Attack to keep going.
